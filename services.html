How we work

Our search process

From understanding the requirement to supporting onboarding, we follow a structured recruitment process.

STEP 01

Understand

Understand the client, open requirements, skills needed and search challenges through discussions with HR and stakeholders.

STEP 02

Source candidates

Use available channels and targeted searches by industry, geography, function and cost; validate candidate interest.

STEP 03

Interview & coordinate

Conduct preliminary interviews, submit profiles and coordinate discussions or assessments with the client.

STEP 04

Offer & onboard

Support document collection, references, offer clarification, joining timelines and onboarding formalities.
